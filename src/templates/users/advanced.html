Sync All Tracked Media

Refreshes titles, artwork, and release years for every tracked item from their original providers — the same thing the “Sync” button on a detail page does, just for your whole library at once.
Runs automatically once a month, but you can trigger it manually here. Can take a while for large libraries since it goes through the same rate limiters as everything else — check the container logs for progress.

Clear Search Cache

Yamtrack stores your search results in a cache to speed up performance and reduce API calls.
Normally, these cached results expire automatically after 24 hours. If your search results appear outdated, you can clear the cache manually.
Your next search may be slightly slower as fresh data is retrieved.
