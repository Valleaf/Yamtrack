Import from SensCritique

SensCritique protects its API with Cloudflare, so we use a bookmarklet that runs directly in your browser while you're logged in.

1

Add the bookmarklet to your browser

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:

⭐ Import to Yamtrack

Can't drag? Right-click the button → Bookmark this link

2

Go to SensCritique and log in

Open SensCritique →
3

Click the bookmarklet

A progress overlay will appear. It fetches your entire collection and sends it to Yamtrack automatically.

4

Check your import history

Come back here to see the results once the ✅ appears.
